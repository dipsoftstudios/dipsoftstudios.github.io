A SMALL INDEPENDENT STUDIO

Built with care,
one useful idea at a time.

Dip Soft Studios is an independent effort by Sudip Bhattacharjya in India. I want to make tools that solve ordinary problems without making people work around clutter or complexity.

PataHive is the first step: one place to keep bookmarks, notes, documents, shopping items, and things to watch. It is shaped by real use and improved gradually, with the aim of making a capable personal workspace available without putting its core usefulness out of reach.

Keeping software accessible takes time for design, development, testing, and distribution. If an app is useful to you and you’re able, optional support helps me keep improving it and work on future ideas. Support is never required to download PataHive or use the app.
