REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Fair, clear
from the start.

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains current free downloads and optional support, and how paid digital products will be handled if introduced.

1. Current downloads and contributions

PataHive is offered as a free Android download through its official GitHub releases page. This website does not sell an app, subscription, or other digital product.

International contributions are voluntary and are started on an external payment provider’s page. If you made a contribution by mistake or need help with a transaction, use the contact form with the date and transaction reference. Any refund controls available for that contribution are subject to the provider’s terms and your payment method. The India contribution option is planned and is not active.

2. Future paid digital products

If we add a paid app, feature, or other digital product, its price, inclusions, delivery method, cancellation window, refund eligibility, and expected refund timeline will be displayed before checkout. A purchase may be cancelled or refunded if required by applicable law or the specific offer terms.

We will publish the specific cancellation and refund terms before accepting such payment. Until then, there is no purchase or recurring charge to cancel through this site.

3. How to request help

Use the contact form and include the product or contribution, transaction date, and payment reference. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or payment credentials.
