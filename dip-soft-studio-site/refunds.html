REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Fair, clear
from the start.

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains current free downloads and optional support, and how paid digital products will be handled if introduced.

1. Current downloads and contributions

PataHive is currently offered as a free Android download through its official GitHub release. This website does not sell an app, subscription, or other digital product, and does not currently offer Razorpay checkout.

Ko-fi contributions are voluntary support payments initiated on Ko-fi. If you contributed by mistake or need help with a transaction, contact dipsoftstudios@gmail.com with the date and transaction reference. Payment processing and any available refund controls are subject to Ko-fi’s terms and your payment method.

2. Future paid digital products

If we add a paid app, feature, or other digital product, its price, inclusions, delivery method, cancellation window, refund eligibility, and expected refund timeline will be displayed before checkout. A purchase may be cancelled or refunded if required by applicable law or the specific offer terms. Refunds for a Razorpay transaction, if Razorpay is enabled, will be initiated to the original payment method where supported by the provider.

We will publish the specific cancellation and refund terms before accepting such payment. Until then, there is no Razorpay purchase or recurring charge to cancel through this site.

3. How to request help

Email dipsoftstudios@gmail.com and include the product or contribution, transaction date, and payment reference. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or payment credentials.
