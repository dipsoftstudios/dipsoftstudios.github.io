Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This page covers this website. For information about data inside the PataHive Android app, read the PataHive Privacy Policy.

1. About this site

This website is operated by Dip Soft Studios, the studio name used by Sudip Bhattacharjya in India. You can contact the studio through the contact page.

2. Information collected by the site

This static website does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics, or a newsletter signup. It does not set analytics or advertising cookies. The Support page loads Razorpay’s payment-button script to provide an optional India contribution checkout. If you start a payment, Razorpay processes the information you enter, which may include contact details and payment and transaction information. Razorpay may share customer and transaction details with the studio as described in its policies and your checkout consent. The website does not receive or store your card or bank credentials. The contact form collects the name, reply address, topic, and message you submit. FormSubmit processes and forwards the message to the studio; its service may retain submissions for up to 30 days. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information through the contact form. When you visit, the hosting provider may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser, requested page, and request time in its server logs for delivery, security, and reliability. The selected hosting provider’s terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

3. External links and payment providers

The International support option opens Ko-fi. The India support option uses Razorpay’s embedded checkout, which loads payment functionality from Razorpay. GitHub, Ko-fi, Razorpay, FormSubmit, and other linked or embedded services process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Dip Soft Studios does not control their privacy practices. Read the Razorpay Privacy Policy and Ko-fi Privacy Policy for details.

4. Data retention and your choices

FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days; the message delivered to the studio inbox may remain there until deleted. Razorpay controls retention of checkout and transaction information under its policies and applicable requirements. Hosting logs, if retained, are controlled by the selected hosting provider and its retention settings. You can use your browser’s privacy controls to manage third-party content and cookies; blocking the Razorpay script may prevent the India checkout from working.

5. Changes and contact

This policy may change if the site gains new services. The current version and effective date are shown here. Questions can be sent using the contact form.
