This page covers this website. For information about data inside the PataHive Android app, read the PataHive Privacy Policy.

This website is operated by Dip Soft Studios, the studio name used by Sudip Bhattacharjya in India. You can contact the studio through the contact page.

2. Information collected by the site

This static website does not provide user accounts, checkout, advertising, analytics, or a newsletter signup. It does not set analytics or advertising cookies. If you use the contact form, it collects the name, reply address, topic, and message you submit. FormSubmit processes and forwards the message to the studio; its service may retain submissions for up to 30 days. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information. When you visit, the hosting provider may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser, requested page, and request time in its server logs for delivery, security, and reliability. The selected hosting provider’s terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

3. External links

Links to GitHub, the international support provider, FormSubmit, and other services take you to third-party services. Those services process data under their own policies. Dip Soft Studios does not control their privacy practices.

4. Data retention and your choices

FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days; the message delivered to the studio inbox may remain there until deleted. Hosting logs, if retained, are controlled by the selected hosting provider and its retention settings. You can use your browser’s privacy controls to limit cookies, though the site does not require cookies for its core pages.

5. Changes and contact

This policy may change if the site gains new services. The current version and effective date are shown here. Questions can be sent using the contact form.
