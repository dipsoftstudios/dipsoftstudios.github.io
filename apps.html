More about PataHive

PataHive brings the useful things you want to keep into one personal workspace. Save links and bookmarks with tags, keep notes and attachments together, organise documents, manage a shopping list, and collect films or shows for later. Search and filter your saved items when you need them, then share or export content using Android’s built-in tools. Your library is designed for your own device and routines, so everyday references stay easy to find without scattering them across separate apps.
